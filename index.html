About the Diversity Visa (DV) Program

Question 1: What are the requirements for the DV program?

Education or work experience: The principal applicant generally must have completed a U.S.-equivalent high-school education, meaning successful completion of a formal course of elementary and secondary education comparable to 12 years in the United States, or have qualifying work experience under the official rules. The work-experience option generally requires at least two years of experience within the past five years in an occupation that requires at least two years of training or experience. Not every job qualifies.

Photo: Each required photograph must meet the current official DV photo specifications. EXIE can offer photo assistance, but applicants should check the official requirements.

Country of eligibility: Eligibility is generally based on country of birth, subject to the official rules and exceptions. A spouse or child may be chargeable to the principal applicant's eligible country under the program rules. Check the instructions for the applicable year.

Question 2: Which children must I include in my DV entry?

Under the DV rules, a qualifying child is generally unmarried and under 21. Required children can include your biological children, qualifying stepchildren, and children legally adopted by you, even if they do not live with you or do not plan to immigrate. Follow the current official instructions for the exact categories and exceptions.

Do not list grandchildren, nieces, nephews, or other children as your own qualifying children unless they meet the official definition, including applicable legal-adoption rules. Omitting a child who must be listed can lead to disqualification or visa refusal.

Question 3: How many times can I enter the DV program each year?

Single person: Each person may submit only one entry as the principal entrant during each registration period. Submitting more than one entry for the same person can lead to disqualification.

Married couple: If both spouses independently meet the requirements, each spouse may submit one separate entry as the principal entrant. Each entry must correctly list the other spouse and all required children. This can give the couple two entries in total, but each person must not submit multiple entries as principal entrant. Follow the official instructions for the current year.

More Questions About EXIE and Applying

Question 4: Is EXIE a U.S. government office?

No. EXIE is an independent private business and is not affiliated with or acting on behalf of the U.S. Department of State or the U.S. Embassy in Monrovia.

Question 5: Can EXIE guarantee that I will be selected?

No. DV selection is determined by the official process. No service provider can guarantee selection, visa approval, or a successful interview.

Question 6: Does it cost money to enter the DV lottery?

The official DV entry is free when you complete it yourself through the official website. EXIE may charge for optional private assistance. Confirm the service fee before paying.

Question 7: If I am in 12th grade, can I apply?

You may submit an entry if you meet the program requirements. However, you must meet the education or qualifying work-experience requirement by the time of your visa interview. Check the official instructions to confirm the requirements that apply to you.

Question 8: If I am a foreigner living in Liberia, can I apply here?

Living in Liberia does not by itself determine your country of eligibility. Eligibility is generally based on country of birth, subject to official rules and exceptions. Do not automatically choose Liberia if you were born elsewhere; check the current official instructions.

Question 9: My children are out of the country or out of town. Can I apply without them?

You generally must list all eligible children required by the official instructions, even if they live elsewhere. Follow the current photo and information requirements for each child. Omitting a required child or giving inaccurate information may lead to disqualification or visa refusal.

Question 10: Can I apply as married without my spouse?

If you are legally married, you generally must include your spouse in the entry, even if they live elsewhere. Follow the official requirements for their information and photograph. Do not list yourself as single or unmarried if you are legally married. If your spouse does not want to immigrate, that does not automatically mean you should omit them. Check the current official instructions for applicable exceptions.

Where should I check my DV entry status?

Use the official Entrant Status Check at dvprogram.state.gov. Keep your confirmation details safe.

Careful Data Entry & Customer Review

We aim to make each entry process clear, careful, and transparent. Customers should have the opportunity to check their information before it is submitted.

✓

Steps we follow to help reduce errors

EXIE says it is equipped to serve up to 500 principal entrants per day. Actual daily capacity may depend on staffing and demand. Our process is designed to give customers time to participate and review their details.

Enter information together

After data entry, customers are given an opportunity to review the information carefully and point out corrections before submission.

03

Confirm details and consent

Customers should verify that the information is accurate and understand what information is being submitted. Never approve an entry you have not reviewed.

CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Entry Information Backup — With Your Consent

With a customer's informed consent, EXIE may keep a backup copy of submitted entry information for two stated purposes:

  • Recovery: to help the customer recover information if it is misplaced later.
  • Future follow-up: to help the customer locate their saved details when checking official results and preparing for next steps.

Your information matters: Before agreeing, ask how your information will be protected, who can access it, how long it will be kept, and how you can request its deletion. Backups are not a substitute for keeping your own confirmation number and records safe.

EXIE should only store personal information with clear consent and appropriate safeguards. Result checks and any further processing must follow the official program instructions.
